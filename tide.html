INTRADAY PATTERN RESEARCH

Tide looks for repeatable moves inside the trading day.

It tests defined entry patterns, measures what prices do afterward, and compares exit rules on data kept separate for development, validation, and a final holdout.

What is already built

Setup research

Ten candidate rules cover VWAP reclaim and reversion, opening range, volume breakout, and consolidation patterns in both directions, using 1, 5, and 15-minute signals.

Exits and validation

Measures forward returns and favorable/adverse excursions, compares stop and target rules, and separates development, validation, and final test months. Matched random entries provide a baseline.

Execution bridge

Prepared signals can run through Horizon’s intraday simulator, which models prior-minute quotes, costs, gaps, session exits, and conservative handling when a bar touches both stop and target.

What is needed before a study can run, and the path to findings

Needed first

  • At least 12 months of regular-session trades and consolidated quotes for a point-in-time large-cap universe, plus SPY and QQQ (WRDS TAQ covers this).
  • Clean timestamps and a consistent ticker-to-PERMNO map, with trade corrections and sale conditions handled before import.
  • CRSP prices and S&P 500 membership refreshed to cover the study window.
  • Recorded cost assumptions; short setups also need historical borrow evidence.

Path

  1. Sample exportOne session, a few symbols including SPY, to confirm columns and quote coverage.
  2. Clean and mapMinute bars with valid NBBO, then identity, corporate-action and session checks.
  3. Bounded studySignals, excursions and exit rules compared with costs.
  4. Unseen periodsMatched baselines, walk-forward and final holdout.
  5. Review and paper tradeCandidates are labelled for manual review; only those passing predefined checks move to paper.
