EVERY IDEA TESTED

Every idea we have tested, counted in one place.

Each experiment checks its own versions for luck. That isn't enough: test enough ideas and one of them will look good by chance. So every idea tried anywhere in the project is counted here (Compass experiments, Theta's options tests, and the side studies), and a new result is judged against all of them.
