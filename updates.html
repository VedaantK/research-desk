October 22, 2026

An overnight research run: small caps and the quarterly gap

  • Ran 16 sealed backtests overnight, three engines each, rules fixed before any results.
  • Explained part of why the old quarterly small-cap screen looked better than the annual study: its own selection rules recover about half the difference, but still fail and lose half their edge at higher trading costs. Details.
  • Tested five small-cap ideas as 20-stock portfolios a small account could hold. All five failed: none had an edge beyond known factors, and their worst drops were 56–88%. Details.
  • Lesson: published small-cap factors work as broad portfolios of hundreds of stocks; the 20 most extreme names add risk, not edge.
  • Added a Research notes index, so each sealed study has its own page.
  • One run was rejected because two engines disagreed by two millionths of a share; that is an engine rounding issue to fix, not a strategy result.

October 21, 2026

Compared the controls and recorded the conclusion

  • Published four more tests: value alone and profitability alone, each with normal and doubled trading costs. All three engines agreed.
  • Value alone returned 5.85% a year with a 59.67% worst fall. Profitability alone returned 8.75% with a 42.59% worst fall.
  • The original combination returned 8.47% with a 46.64% worst fall. All three portfolios failed the unchanged advancement criteria.
  • Profitability alone improved return by only 0.28 percentage points and beat the combination in 11 of 22 years. That is not a validated new winner.
  • Doubled costs reduced annual returns by 0.15–0.23 percentage points. Costs are not the main explanation for the failed tests.
  • Added comparison charts, all six normal/stressed portfolio results, yearly returns, and explicit listing-gap warnings.
  • Next research improvements: add a benchmark from the same eligible stocks, diagnose concentration and drawdowns, and audit data timing and gap exits. No follow-up strategy tests were launched for this review.
  • Industry-neutral tests need historical industry labels; first-reported validation needs historical fundamental vintages. Those inputs are absent from the sealed research data.

October 21, 2026

The annual research batch is complete

  • Finished all seven scheduled tests: the baseline, higher costs, two value cutoffs and three historical subperiods.
  • All three backtesting engines agreed on every run. The baseline reproduced the earlier equity result exactly.
  • The baseline returned 8.47% a year after modeled costs, with a Sharpe ratio of 0.58 and a worst fall of 46.64%.
  • It failed two advancement checks: the 35% drawdown limit and the factor-alpha statistical threshold.
  • Higher modeled costs reduced annualized returns to 8.28%. Nearby cutoffs returned 7.76%–8.82%; none was picked as a new winner.
  • Execution is complete. The current strategy has not earned advancement, and no fresh holdout was opened.
