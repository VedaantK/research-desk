October 21, 2026

The annual research batch is complete

  • Finished all seven scheduled tests: the baseline, higher costs, two value cutoffs and three historical subperiods.
  • All three backtesting engines agreed on every run. The baseline reproduced the earlier equity result exactly.
  • The baseline returned 8.47% a year after modeled costs, with a Sharpe ratio of 0.58 and a worst fall of 46.64%.
  • It failed two advancement checks: the 35% drawdown limit and the factor-alpha statistical threshold.

  • Execution is complete. The current strategy has not earned advancement, and no fresh holdout was opened.
