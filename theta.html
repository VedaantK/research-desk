OPTIONS RESEARCH

Theta tests options strategies against real trading costs.

Every idea is scored before it is tested: published evidence, who pays the premium and why, edge after a post-publication haircut, and edge measured against the bid-ask spread we would actually pay. Strategies that pass are pre-registered and run on OptionMetrics data through an engine checked against Cboe's published indexes.

Is selling options paying right now?

A daily readout, not a tested strategy. VAY (volatility-adjusted yield, an idea from DefineYourRisk) is the price of a one-month at-the-money call divided by what that call is worth if the stock keeps moving as it did over the last 21 trading days: 1.00 is fair, above 1 means sellers are paid more than recent movement justifies. IVx is 30-day implied volatility, IV rank where it sits in its one-year range (0–100), IV percentile the share of the past year's days it was lower. Highlighted rows have IV rank ≥ 50 and VAY ≥ 1.2. Our own test of the implied-minus-realized trade did not earn twice its trading costs, so a rich reading is a starting point, not a signal.

Edge versus cost

Long-short straddle strategies pay the spread on both sides every month. k is the share of the quoted half-spread paid away from the midpoint: 0 is a midpoint fill, 1 is the full bid or ask. A strategy must earn at least twice its cost. Edges are the papers' own gross estimates, not our returns.

How an options idea gets tested, and limits

The intake gates

  1. EvidencePublished in a peer-reviewed journal and shown to survive outside its sample.
  2. MechanismWe can name who is on the other side and why they keep paying.
  3. Haircut edgeThe published edge × 0.42 (returns fall about 58% after publication) is still positive.
  4. Edge ÷ cost ≥ 2Using fill costs measured from 1.56 million real option trades.
  5. Power and replicationEnough data to reach t = 3, and the paper's own result reproduced first.
  6. Account fitMargin, worst-case loss per position and contract size at a realistic account size.
