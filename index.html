ATLAS · RESEARCH DESK

Three ways of finding out whether a trading idea is real.

Each one is held to the same rule: write down what counts as success before looking, record every attempt, and publish the failures next to the wins. They are kept apart because they test different things on different data.

01 · Backtests

Systematic strategies on the S&P 500

An autonomous loop (Atlas) turns plain-English ideas into code and tests them on survivorship-free daily data. Horizon’s three engines must agree on every fill. Strategies that clear fixed gates get a one-time holdout, then paper trading.

How they fit together

Shared referee

Horizon is the backtester under both Atlas and Tide. It refuses a result unless independent engines reconcile.

Rules before results

Gates, trial budgets and holdouts are fixed in advance. Beacon’s are hash-chained and frozen before any rating is made.

Honest status

A page says “no results yet” when that is true. Nothing here is investment advice.
